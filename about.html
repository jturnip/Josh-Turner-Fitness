Josh Turner

I started lifting weights at 15 years old — an underweight kid constantly told, “You’re just naturally skinny. That’s just your genetics.” But something clicked after watching a few motivational videos. I discovered a passion that would change my life: the power to transform myself. In my first month of training, I gained 12 pounds. I was still skinny — just less skinny — but I saw hope. Six months in, I had put on 22 pounds and for the first time, I could look in the mirror and see some muscle. Over the past 8 years, I’ve dedicated myself to training hard and learning everything I can about natural muscle growth — testing, refining, and applying strategies to add size. I’ve gained over 50 pounds of lean mass, not because of shortcuts, but because of relentless consistency, research, and a deep belief that great effort will lead to great results. If I had known back then what I know now, my progress would’ve been even faster. But that’s the beauty of the journey: learning, growing, and using what you’ve learned to become the best version of yourself. Now, my mission is to help others do the same. I specialize in helping naturally skinny guys build real muscle and confidence — without gimmicks or guesswork. If you’re ready to train with purpose, fuel your body properly, and transform from skinny to jacked, I’m here to guide you every step of the way. Let’s build the strongest version of you.
